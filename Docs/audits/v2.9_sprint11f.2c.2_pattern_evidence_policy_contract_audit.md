# V2.9 Sprint 11F.2C.2 Pattern Evidence Policy Contract Audit
**Status:** PATTERN EVIDENCE POLICY CONTRACT VERIFIED — READY FOR 11F.2C IMPLEMENTATION

## 1. Scope of Audit
This audit verifies the codification of the V1 Evidence Sufficiency policy into the permanent contracts (`DATA_CONTRACT.md`, `AI_BEHAVIOR_CONTRACT.md`, `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md`). No implementation (Kotlin code) or tests were modified during this phase.

## 2. Policy Verification Checklist

### 1. Minimum Evidence
**Verified:** Codified in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` Section 331.
A minimum of 3 unique valid `EvidenceReference` items `(sourceId, sourceType)` is required. Duplicate references do not count toward this threshold.

### 2. Minimum Distinct Calendar Days
**Verified:** Codified in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` Section 331.
Valid evidence must span at least 2 distinct calendar days based on calendar date, not millisecond elapsed time.

### 3. Timezone Authority
**Verified:** Codified in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` Section 331 and `AI_BEHAVIOR_CONTRACT.md` Section 280.
The timezone used to determine calendar-day boundaries must be supplied by the domain execution context and is explicitly not AI-controlled.

### 4. Source Diversity
**Verified:** Codified in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` Section 331.
Source diversity is explicitly not required for V1. Any mix of EVENT, OBSERVATION, or REFLECTION may independently satisfy the requirement without source-type weighting.

### 5. Contradiction Signal
**Verified:** Codified in `AI_BEHAVIOR_CONTRACT.md` Section 280.
`PatternCandidate.contradictionSignal` remains an AI-generated hypothesis. It must not independently block Evidence Sufficiency, nor mutate or expire a Pattern.

### 6. AI Confidence
**Verified:** Codified in `AI_BEHAVIOR_CONTRACT.md` Section 280.
AI-generated confidence must not contribute to Evidence Sufficiency and remains hypothesis metadata only.

### 7. Pattern Confidence
**Verified:** Codified in `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md` Section 220.
Initial Pattern confidence is explicitly deferred to a separate future Pattern Confidence contract. V1 Evidence Sufficiency must not calculate or guess Pattern confidence.

### 8. Persistence Boundary
**Verified:** Codified in `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md` Section 220 and `DATA_CONTRACT.md` Section 331.
Evidence Sufficiency must not persist Pattern. It acts solely as an evaluation gate that yields a domain result for acceptance flows to consume.

### 9. INSUFFICIENT vs ERROR Semantics
**Verified:** Codified in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` Section 331 and `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md` Section 220.
The result is a structured domain result (`SUFFICIENT`, `INSUFFICIENT`, `ERROR`). Valid evidence below thresholds results in `INSUFFICIENT`, while repository/infrastructure failures result in `ERROR`. Failures must not be coerced into "insufficient evidence".

### 10. Existing Pattern Decay
**Verified:** Codified in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` Section 331.
Decay remains entirely separate from Sufficiency. It applies to already-authoritative Patterns and maintains the existing 30-day threshold, 0.5 factor, and < 0.1 expiration limit.

### 11. Privacy Boundary
**Verified:** Codified in `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md` Section 220.
Sufficiency evaluates only the minimum necessary metadata via identity (`sourceId`, `sourceType`) and timestamps. It does not require or log raw payload text, content, or coordinates.

### 12. No Contradictory Existing Contract
**Verified:** Previous read-only audits (11F.2C.1) demonstrated that there were no conflicting pre-existing thresholds, confidence generation formulas, or temporal policies for Pattern creation in the codebase or contracts.

### 13. No Unrelated Changes
**Verified:** Edits were strictly limited to appending the V1 Evidence Sufficiency requirements to the appropriate permanent contract documents.
